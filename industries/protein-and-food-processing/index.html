04.1.1
Protein and food processing

Process it where the feed is grown.

Pork, dairy, poultry and plant protein. Manitoba produces roughly 29 percent of Canada's hogs and is Western Canada's dairy capital. The raw material, the barns and the freight are here. The region is short on the processing that captures the value.

Talk about a processing project
04.1.2
The pattern

HyLife exports premium pork to Japan from a village of a few hundred people.

That is what this region means when it says specialized companies become world-class. You do not need a port city or a head office tower to sell into one of the most demanding food markets on earth. You need the feedstock, the freight, the inspection culture and people who will stay.

A processor moving here inherits all four on day one, plus a supplier base that has already passed the audits yours will face.

04.1.3
The investment case

47 percent of Canada's live-pig exports leave from Manitoba.

They leave as animals, and the margin gets added somewhere else. Every step taken here is value that stays in the province.

04.1.8
The constraint

Water and wastewater decide wet processing.

For a water-intensive process in this region these two lines decide your project. Take the file, and take it early.

Water

Natural spring water from the regional water table. It has supplied this region's processors for generations. Ask for the current assay, quality profile and available volumes.

Wastewater

Capacity is constrained and it is the region's active infrastructure project. Ask for this month's status.

Raise a water-intensive process in the first conversation. It will either work here or it will not, and we would both rather know in week one.
