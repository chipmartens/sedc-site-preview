04.2.1
Advanced manufacturing

Loewen, Barkman and WS Steel already ship to export spec from here.

Windows in high-end homes across North America. Concrete in streetscapes across Canada. Tier 1 OEM steel fabrication. This region's manufacturers compete at export standard from Manitoba's third-largest city, and the supplier base is tuned accordingly.

Talk about a plant
04.2.2
The pattern

Loewen sells into the top of the North American window market from a city of about 18,000.

Premium windows are an unforgiving category. The tolerances are tight, the finish is visible, and the customer is often an architect. Loewen has held that position from here for decades, and Barkman and WS Steel hold comparable positions in theirs.

What that means for you is the supplier base underneath them. Your fabricators, your finishers and your trades already work to that standard every week.

04.2.3
The advantage

Your local suppliers already know what your tolerance means.

A precision manufacturer moving into a new region usually spends two years teaching local suppliers what tolerance means.

Loewen has run a premium window line here for decades. Barkman ships concrete product nationally. WS Steel runs a highly automated Tier 1 OEM fabrication operation. The expectation is already set, and you inherit it.

04.2.6
What ships from here

Barkman hardscape leaves this region for streetscapes across Canada.

A paver is an unglamorous object with an unforgiving spec. It has to hold colour, hold dimension through a Manitoba freeze-thaw cycle, and arrive on a pallet without a chipped edge.

The plants that hit that standard here are the same plants your suppliers would be.

04.2.7
Power

Manitoba Hydro rates, and a climate that helps.

Manitoba's industrial electricity rates are among the lowest in North America and the supply is predominantly hydroelectric. For an energy-intensive line that is a structural cost position rather than a one-year incentive.

The cold season also carries part of your heat rejection. Request the current rate schedule and we will walk your load through it with you.
