04.4.1
Transportation and logistics

One hour to CentrePort, with third-generation carriers already on the road.

One hour to CentrePort Canada. One hour to the United States. Third-generation carriers with national and cross-border authority already running from here.

Talk about a freight or storage project
04.4.2
The pattern

Penner is one of Canada's leading van carriers, third generation, run from here.

Big Freight has seven decades behind it. Steve's Livestock built a specialty around the protein cluster it serves. None of them relocated to a bigger market to get there.

For a shipper that means the carrier capacity here was built by people who answer to customers on the same road, and a new entrant competes on service against a high bar rather than filling a vacuum.

04.4.5
Why it matters

Food freight runs on temperature, welfare, timing and paperwork.

Carriers here have been hauling for this cluster for generations.

Your logistics risk on day one is lower than it would be in a region where you are the first perishable shipper on the road teaching a local carrier what a reefer audit is.
