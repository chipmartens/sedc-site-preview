07.5
Utilities and inputs

Every claim, with the evidence behind it.

Open any one for the source and the caveat.

Natural spring water

There is no river here you can photograph, and any brochure that says abundant water without explaining itself deserves your suspicion. What this region has is natural spring water drawn from the regional water table, which has supplied its processors for generations.

Ask for the current assay, quality profile and available volumes and we will send the file. If your process is water-intensive, raise it in the first conversation.

Industrial power among the lowest cost in North America

Manitoba Hydro supply is predominantly hydroelectric and Manitoba's industrial rates are among the lowest on the continent. For an energy-intensive line that is a structural cost position rather than a one-year incentive that expires.

Request the current rate schedule and we will walk your load through it.

Cold-climate free cooling

For any process carrying a heat-rejection load the climate does part of the work for a meaningful stretch of the year. Combined with the power rate it changes the operating-cost model for cooling-heavy processing.

A direct path to permanent residency

The Rural Community Immigration Pilot lets designated employers in this region hire internationally and put the candidate on a direct path to permanent residency. Steinbach opens a new intake on the first business day of each month and closes it at 7 applications.

Eastman Immigrant Services has settled more than 4,000 families from 100 countries here in the past decade, which is the part that makes people stay after they arrive.

07.6
The honest read

Strengths and constraints in one table.

A region that hides its constraints will surprise you after you have signed. Here are ours, in the same table as the strengths.

STRONG
  • ++Feedstock and cluster densityRoughly 29 percent of Canada's hogs, Western Canada's dairy capital, processors and carriers inside one hour.
  • ++AccessOne hour to a trimodal inland port and Foreign Trade Zone, one hour to the United States, cargo airport within 100 km.
  • ++PowerAmong the lowest industrial electricity rates in North America, predominantly hydroelectric.
  • ++The hiring routeA direct path to permanent residency through a designated employer here.
  • ++Cost of livingMaterially below the major metros most relocating staff come from. This is the line that makes hiring work.
CONSTRAINED
  • !!Serviced industrial landLimited. Ask for the current serviced and unserviced breakdown before you model a timeline.
  • !!Wastewater capacityConstrained, and the region's active infrastructure file. Ask for this month's status.
  • !!Applied research inside the clusterThe feedstock and the processors are here. The applied food science is elsewhere.
  • !!Cold storage and packagingThin relative to processing volume. Currently bought outside the region.
