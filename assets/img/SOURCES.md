## The rule

Landscapes standing in for a region are a defensible placeholder in a staging
build. **Generated faces presented as the local workforce are not.** They are
the single thing a board member or a resident would spot first, and on a site
whose whole argument is evidence, getting caught with a synthetic welder would
cost more than the photography budget.

Shoot the three portraits first. Everything else on this list can wait.
