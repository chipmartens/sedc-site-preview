02.2
The constraint

The orders are there. The shifts are short.

Ask around this region and the story repeats. The orders are there. The line could run longer. The drawing for the second building has been sitting for two years.

What is missing is a welder, a site, a program application nobody has time to fill out, or a municipal conversation that keeps getting pushed. That is the work we do.

02.3
The service

Hiring, sites, program applications and press.

  • 01
    Talent, including immigration
    The regional pilot moves a candidate to a direct path to permanent residency against We help you get designated, write the job offers, and work with Eastman Immigrant Services so the family stays.
  • 02
    Room to expand
    Serviced and unserviced sites, buildings, and the municipal conversation about what it takes to get from one to the other.
  • 03
    Funding you did not have time to find
    Provincial and federal programs for automation, training, export development and capital. We track what is open and tell you when something fits.
  • 04
    We make your win loud
    When you land something we put it in front of media, the province and the companies we are trying to attract. Your growth is the strongest recruiting argument this region has.
