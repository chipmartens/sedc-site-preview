03.1
Work here

A real job at an exporting company, and a house you can own.

Southeast Manitoba is hiring trades, operators, engineers, healthcare and professional staff at companies that sell to the world. If you are coming from outside Canada the regional pilot can bring you to a direct path to permanent residency.

03.2
The pathway

How the immigration pilot actually works.

The Rural Community Immigration Pilot lets designated employers in this region hire internationally and put you on a direct path to permanent residency.

SOURCE: STEINBACHEDC.COM/RCIP. SEDC PUBLISHES NO PROCESSING TIME FOR THIS PILOT AND NEITHER DO WE.

03.3
The steps

The four steps, start to permanent residency.

  • 01
    A designated employer offers you a job
    Only a designated employer can support an application, and only in a priority occupation. The current list is public: see the designated employers (PDF).
  • 02
    The employer requests a recommendation from the community
    That request comes to this office and a real person reviews it.
  • 03
    You apply for permanent residency
    With the recommendation attached, your file moves on the regional timeline.
  • 04
    Your family lands
    Eastman Immigrant Services helps with housing, schools, language, banking and the first winter. They have done this for more than 4,000 families.

03.7
Living here

What it costs and what is in town.

01

Ownership is in reach

This is the part people move for. Housing here is priced for a single income at a trade wage, which is untrue in most of the places people are leaving.

02

Under an hour to a city

Winnipeg is close enough for a concert, a flight or a specialist appointment, and far enough that you are paying small-city prices to live.

03

Healthcare and school in town

Bethesda Regional Health Centre is the regional hospital. RRC Polytech has a Steinbach campus. Hanover School Division and Steinbach Bible College are here.

04

You will not be the first

More than 100 nationalities already work here. Eastman Immigrant Services has settled over 4,000 families from 100 countries in the past decade and has won awards for it.

03.8
Moving within Canada

Many people here are coming back.

A lot of the people moving into this region already have a cousin, a parent or a church on the same road. The jobs are real, the housing math works, and the community was built by people who arrived with nothing and stayed.
