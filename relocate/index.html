01.1
Relocate or expand

Operate inside a supply chain that is already running.

If you process, manufacture, move or supply anything that starts on a field, the feed mills, the carriers, the food-grade contractors and the trades are already here. Assembling that from nothing takes about three years.

01.2
The short list

Land price, tax rate and power cost converge once you are down to three finalists.

What separates the last three is who answers the phone, how fast the answer comes back, and whether the companies around the site can actually supply you.

Scott Rickey answers, usually within a business day, and he will put you in a room with the operators who already buy what you would be buying.

01.3
Day one

What exists here before you build anything.

01

Feedstock and supply

Manitoba produces roughly 29 percent of Canada's hogs and is Western Canada's dairy capital. The feed, the barns and the primary producers sit inside your delivery radius.

02

Freight

One hour to CentrePort Canada, the largest trimodal inland port and Foreign Trade Zone in North America. One hour to the United States border. Richardson International within 100 km for cargo.

03

Trades and operators

A workforce raised on manufacturing and processing, median age 36, with more than 100 nationalities already working here.

04

Contractors who have already built to food grade

Design-build contractors, steel fabricators, equipment dealers, packaging and waste handlers who have built for HyLife, Loewen and Barkman and know what a food-grade plant needs.

Suppliers

01.7
Fit

Bring us protein processing, precision manufacturing or agri-tech.

Hyperscale data centres, absentee-owned commodity plants and anything drawing a large share of the water table for a handful of jobs get referred somewhere else.

If you land in the poor-fit row you will hear it on the first call, along with the name of a region that fits you better.

  • 01
    A good fit
    Protein and food processing. Advanced and precision manufacturing. Agri-tech and applied research. Packaging, automation and freight, and the suppliers those need.
  • 02
    A poor fit
    Hyperscale data centres. Absentee-owned commodity operations. Anything consuming a large share of scarce water or power for a small number of jobs.
  • 03
    Wide open
    Value-added processing, cold storage, byproduct and rendering capacity, applied food science. Ask us who is currently trucking these in.
