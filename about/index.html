09.1
About

A regional economic development office funded by the companies it serves.

Southeast Manitoba Economic Development is backed by about thirty stakeholder companies from across this region, with a board made up of the people who run them.

09.2
The structure

The people on our board run companies here.

This is a privately funded regional office rather than a municipal department with an economic development mandate attached to it. It is funded by the region's employers, governed by them, and answerable to them.

When we tell you what the labour market is like, it is because the people hiring in it are in the room.

09.4
The promise

We answer our phones.

A large region gives you a portal and a queue. Here you get one economic development officer, one phone number, and a reply from a person, usually within a business day.

Companies choosing a site cannot wait a week for an answer, and they remember who made them wait.

09.5
The team

Who you will actually deal with.

Scott Rickey is the person you email. Gwen Reimer handles immigration and the community recommendation. The rest of the board runs companies in this region.

09.6
The region

Why this stopped being a city office.

Southeast Manitoba, anchored by Steinbach and including the surrounding rural municipalities and communities.

This organisation was a city economic development office and is now a regional one, because the cluster it serves never respected the city limit. The feed comes from one municipality, the plant sits in another, the trucks are registered in a third, and the people drive between all of them.
