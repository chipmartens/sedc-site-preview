05.1
The companies

Sixteen companies, the same trades, the same suppliers, the same freight lanes.

The fastest way to understand this region is to look at what has already been built in it. Twelve of the sixteen companies below were started here and are still run from here.

05.2
Flagship

Lynn and Liana build serveware on a resin invented on a Manitoba Hutterite colony.

Lynn and Liana make serveware in Steinbach using EcoPoxy, a bio-based resin made from agricultural oils that was invented on a Manitoba Hutterite colony. The raw material comes from a field. The design came from a kitchen table. The product ships worldwide.

A spreadsheet would never file that under agribusiness. It is exactly the story of this region: take what the land grows, make it better than anyone expects, and stay.
