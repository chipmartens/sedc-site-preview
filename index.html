What gets made here

Windows, pork, concrete, pharmaceuticals and freight all ship from the same forty minutes.

Loewen ships premium windows across North America. HyLife ships pork to Japan. Barkman ships hardscape concrete across Canada. Bausch Health runs pharmaceutical production. Lynn and Liana ship serveware worldwide out of Steinbach.

They hire from the same trades, buy from the same contractors and put freight on the same lanes. Twelve of the sixteen still make their decisions from a head office inside the region.

Companies here get good enough to sell to the world, and then they stay.

Loewen sells into the top of the North American window market. HyLife exports pork to Japan. Barkman ships hardscape across Canada. WS Steel is a Tier 1 OEM fabricator. Bausch Health runs pharmaceutical production. None of those are regional standards, and all of them were reached from here.

Before you sign

What you inherit on day one.

  • 01
    The densest protein cluster in Canada
    Feed, barns, processing, packaging and freight inside one hour. You join a supply chain that already runs.
  • 02
    Owners who live here
    Twelve of the sixteen exporters above make their own decisions locally. A meeting gets set this week.
  • 03
    A federal immigration pilot designated to this region
    A direct path to permanent residency through a designated employer. Eastman Immigrant Services has settled more than 4,000 families from 100 countries in the past decade.
  • 04
    Manitoba Hydro rates, and a cold season that carries part of your cooling load
    Among the lowest industrial electricity rates in North America, predominantly hydroelectric, plus a climate that carries part of your cooling load.
  • 05
    Natural spring water
    Drawn from the regional water table, which has supplied this region's processors for generations. Ask for the assay and the available volumes.
  • 06
    One phone number
    One economic development officer handles the file. A large region gives you a portal and a queue.
00.7
Constraints

Serviced land is limited and wastewater capacity is tight.

You would find both in week two of your due diligence, so here they are in week one.

Serviced land is limited

We will tell you which sites are serviced, which are not, and what it costs to close the difference.

Wastewater capacity is constrained

It is the region's live infrastructure file. Ask where it stands this month and you will get the working number.

Roughly a billion dollars of protein and agri-food capital has been committed in Manitoba since 2018, so the demand for this category is not in question. What this region is still building is serviced capacity, and the people fixing it sit on our board and run companies here.

Communaute francophone

La Broquerie, Sainte-Anne and Richer run in French every day.
